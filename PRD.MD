1. Overview
Purpose

The Landing Page serves as the first impression of Place XP, VIT Chennai's official club website.

Its purpose is to:

Introduce the club professionally.
Establish credibility.
Encourage students to explore the website.
Drive recruitment applications.
Showcase achievements and activities.
Provide navigation to future pages.

The landing page should feel comparable to modern startup or developer community websites with premium UI/UX and engaging interactions.

2. Objectives

The landing page should:

Create a premium first impression.
Clearly communicate what Place XP is.
Increase recruitment interest.
Highlight club achievements.
Promote upcoming events.
Guide users toward exploring the website.
Be fully responsive across all devices.

12. Success Metrics

The landing page is considered successful if:

Visitors immediately understand what Place XP is.
Navigation feels intuitive and polished.
Recruitment CTA is prominently visible and encourages interaction.
The design feels premium and modern.
The page performs well across devices with fast loading and smooth animations.
It creates a strong first impression that reflects the professionalism of the club.
