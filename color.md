⭐ Overall Design Direction

Think of the website as a blend of:

GitHub Education (developer community)
Linear (minimal elegance)
Vercel (dark theme and typography)
Stripe (smooth gradients and storytelling)

Keep the page 90% dark neutrals, 8% white, and 2% orange accents. This restrained use of color will make the orange stand out as the action color while letting your blue brand identity anchor the entire experience. The result will feel far more premium than using the logo's blue everywhere.
